# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Traffic screening and local data review staff who work with Excel traffic records on a Windows desktop. They need to upload local files, configure checkpoint or key-person filters, inspect matches, and export warning workbooks without sending data outside the machine.

## Product Purpose

The tool turns raw vehicle passage spreadsheets into reviewable screening results. Success means users can complete upload, filter configuration, result review, and Excel export with the same business behavior as the original Flask workflow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The interface should feel like a dependable local analysis console, not a marketing site or decorative dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized hero layouts, decorative gradients, dark cyber styling, novelty form controls, and anything that hides dense data behind presentation. Do not make the workflow feel like a public SaaS landing page.

## Design Principles

Keep the workflow visible: upload, configure, review, export should always be easy to locate.

Protect trust in data: tables, counts, selected filters, and risk levels must be explicit and scan-friendly.

Respect local work habits: support large files, repeated use, keyboard-friendly controls, and compact screen layouts.

Separate configuration from results: filters should be clear before execution, and result pages should prioritize review and export.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Chinese UI text, visible focus states, semantic controls, reduced-motion friendly transitions, and risk labels in addition to color.
